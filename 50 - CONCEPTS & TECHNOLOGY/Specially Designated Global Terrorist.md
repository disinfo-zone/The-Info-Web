A Specially Designated Global Terrorist is a person or entity designated under [[Executive Order 13224]], "Blocking Property and Prohibiting Transactions With Persons Who Commit, Threaten To Commit, or Support Terrorism," which President [[George W. Bush]] signed on September 23, 2001, and which was published in the Federal Register two days later.[^1] Designations are made by the [[State Department|Department of State]] or by the Treasury's [[Office of Foreign Assets Control]], which enters the designees on its list of Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons. A designation blocks the designee's property under American jurisdiction; unlike a [[Foreign Terrorist Organization]] designation, it does not by itself bring the criminal prohibition on material support in 18 U.S.C. 2339B.[^2] Presidents have continued to use the order: on January 20, 2025, one designated cartels and other organizations as Foreign Terrorist Organizations and Specially Designated Global Terrorists, and on November 24, 2025, certain chapters of the Muslim Brotherhood.[^1]

### White Supremacist Designations

On April 7, 2020, the State Department designated the [[Russian Imperial Movement]] of Saint Petersburg and three of its leaders, its founder [[Stanislav Vorobyev]], the [[Imperial Legion]] commander [[Denis Gariev]] and the external-relations coordinator [[Nikolay Trushchalov]], under the order as amended by Executive Order 13886, the first time the United States had applied a terrorist designation to a white supremacist organization. The designation rested on the movement's having "provided training for acts of terrorism": two members of the [[Nordic Resistance Movement]] had trained at its [[Partizan]] camp before the [[2017 Gothenburg Neo-Nazi Bombings|bombings in Gothenburg]].[^3] On June 15, 2022, OFAC designated two further supporters of the movement.[^4]

On January 13, 2025, the State Department designated the [[Terrorgram Collective]] and three of its leaders, the Brazilian channel administrator [[Ciro Daniel Amorim Ferreira]], the Croatian [[Noah Licul]] and the South African [[Hendrik-Wahl Muller]]; the Federal Register notice was published on January 15, 2025.[^5][^6] In 2023 the Brazilian researcher [[Michele Prado]] of [[Stop Hate Brasil]] had reported Telegram channels linked to Ferreira to Brazil's intelligence agency, ABIN, and its Ministry of Justice.[^7]
