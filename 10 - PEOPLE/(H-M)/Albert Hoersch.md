Albert Hoersch (died July 1, 1972, aged 81) lived at 417 West Chew Street in the [[Olney, Philadelphia|Olney]] section of [[Philadelphia]], the house later owned by his grandson, the pornographer and fugitive [[R.C. Horsch]], and from 2006 by his great-grandson [[Eugene Horsch]], and searched by the [[Philadelphia Police Department]] and the [[Federal Bureau of Investigation|FBI]] in the [[Olney House Investigation]] of 2026.[^1][^2][^3]

### Family

He was married to [[Helen E. Hoersch|Helen E. Wissman]], who died at the Chew Street house on August 27, 1968; the family belonged to [[Grace United Methodist Church (Philadelphia)|Grace United Methodist Church]], which was invited to her funeral. Their children were Albert Hoersch Jr., [[Raymond C. Hoersch]], and Helen M. Hoersch.[^1][^2] In January 1955 Mr. and Mrs. Albert Hoersch of Philadelphia were reported as recent guests of Mr. and Mrs. Raymond Hoersch in [[Bucks County, Pennsylvania|Bucks County]].[^4]

### Death

He died on July 1, 1972. His funeral service was held at the Schneider Funeral Home, 617-619 West Olney Avenue, and he was buried in [[Hillside Cemetery]], as his wife had been. The *[[The Philadelphia Inquirer|Philadelphia Inquirer]]* reported in 2026 that Eugene Horsch acquired the Chew Avenue house from his father in 2006.[^1][^2][^3]
