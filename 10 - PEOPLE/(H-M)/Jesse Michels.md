Jesse Michels is the creator and host of American Alchemy, a long-form interview channel on [[YouTube]] and a podcast. A February 2, 2023 episode listing of the podcast [[Infinite Loops]], produced by [[O'Shaughnessy Ventures]], describes him as "an investor at [[Thiel Capital]] and the creator and host of 'American Alchemy', a Youtube channel dedicated to highlighting the most heretical thinkers and ideas of our time."[^2]

### Thiel Capital

[[Federal Election Commission]] (FEC) records list one contribution of 2,900 dollars dated March 30, 2022 from a Jesse Michels of [[Los Angeles]] ZIP code 90046 ([[California]]), giving his employer as "Thiel Capital" and his occupation as "Investor." The contribution appears twice in the federal database because it was routed through the conduit [[WinRed]] (committee C00694323): the candidate committee, [[Blake Masters]] for Senate (C00784165), reported it as "earmarked (non-directed) through WinRed" (receipt image 202204159496855525), and WinRed's own report lists it as "earmarked for Blake Masters for Senate" (image 202212159563803397).[^1] Masters, the candidate, served as chief operating officer of Thiel Capital and president of the [[Thiel Foundation]] and was co-author with [[Peter Thiel]] of Zero to One; Thiel funded a super PAC supporting his 2022 Arizona Senate campaign.[^3]

The same name appears on four smaller contributions to [[ActBlue]] (5 dollars on September 19, 2020 and September 27, 2020, both from [[Brooklyn]], 10 dollars on October 31, 2020 from [[New York City|New York]], and 10 dollars on July 22, 2024 from New York, employer "Alpha FMC," occupation "recruiter"), with employers or statuses other than Thiel Capital; no source ties these to the American Alchemy host.[^1] No FEC contribution under the name was found listing Thiel Capital other than the March 30, 2022 entry.[^1]

### American Alchemy

The Infinite Loops listing for the February 2023 episode lists topics including "the transmission theory of consciousness," "Are mushrooms aliens?" and "Nixon, psychedelics & brainwashing," and a reading list including books by [[Robert Anton Wilson]], [[Tom O'Neill]] and [[David McGowan]].[^2] The [[Sky Watcher|Skywatcher]] project's Part I video lists interviews with [[David Grusch]] and Col. [[Karl Nell]], among others.[^4]

No channel sponsor list, production company, Form D, 501(c) return or lobbying filing was located for American Alchemy; a search of EDGAR full-text for "Jesse Michels" and "American Alchemy" returned no Form D, and the Senate lobbying database holds no client of that name.[^5]
