Cecilia T. Hagerty, born Cecilia T. Stock (died October 25, 1991, aged 93), was a licensed practical nurse in [[Stroudsburg, Pennsylvania|Stroudsburg]] and the maternal grandmother of the [[Philadelphia]] pornographer and fugitive [[R.C. Horsch]], who was born near Stroudsburg in 1943.[^1][^2]

She was born in [[Reading, Pennsylvania|Reading]], a daughter of Thomas and Rosanna Stock, and married [[Eugene D. Hagerty]]; in September 1969 the couple's fiftieth wedding anniversary was celebrated in Stroudsburg with their children and grandchildren, among them Mr. and Mrs. [[Raymond C. Hoersch|Raymond Hoersch]] of [[Doylestown, Pennsylvania|Doylestown]] and Mrs. Raymond Hoersch Jr. of Philadelphia. She worked as a nurse at the former Rosenkrans Hospital in Stroudsburg and was one of the first nurses at the Laurel Manor Nursing Home there, and was a member of [[St. John's Lutheran Church (Stroudsburg)|St. John's Lutheran Church]]. She lived on Sarah Street and died at the [[Pocono Medical Center]] in [[East Stroudsburg, Pennsylvania|East Stroudsburg]], survived by her daughter [[Clara Hoersch]] of [[Chalfont, Pennsylvania|Chalfont]], four sons in Berks County and Stroudsburg, a sister, sixteen grandchildren, and great-grandchildren.[^1][^3]
