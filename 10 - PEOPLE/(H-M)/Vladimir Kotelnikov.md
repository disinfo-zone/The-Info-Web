Vladimir Aleksandrovich Kotelnikov was born in [[Kazan]], the son of the mathematician Aleksandr Petrovich Kotelnikov. He was elected a full member of the [[Soviet Academy of Sciences|USSR Academy of Sciences]] in 1953, at 45, without passing through associate membership, and became deputy director and from 1954 director of the Institute of Radio Engineering and Electronics. For about twenty years from 1969 he was vice president and then first vice president of the Academy, coordinating research in communications, radio technology, radio astronomy and space research. He was an honorary member of the IEEE and received the Rein Prize in 1999 and the Bell Gold Medal in 2000. He died on February 11, 2005, in his ninety-seventh year.[^1]

### In the DIA Briefing

A [[Defense Intelligence Agency]] briefing on Soviet psychoenergetics given to the [[United States Air Force|Air Force]] in 1987 quoted "V. A. Kotel'nikov," vice president of the Academy: "Psychokinetic abilities of N. Kulagina are real."[^2] See [[Ninel Kulagina]], [[Yuri Kobzarev]] and [[Psychoenergetics]].
