Alex Klokus (filed as Alexander Klokus and, on one Form D, Alexander Lockwood Klokus) is named in securities filings as an officer or director of [[Futurism, LLC]], [[Theo Rewards, Inc.]], [[Tomorrow Farms Inc.]], [[Shuttle Labs LLC]], [[Radial Health, Inc.]] and [[Microcapital, Inc.]], and as a managing member of the general partners of [[SALT Fund I, L.P.]] and [[Kyber Capital Crypto Fund I LP]] and of their adviser, [[SALT Fund Management LLC]]. Florida records list him as the member and authorized signer of [[ENNEA LLC]], the Delaware company that owns the SKYWATCHER trademark application associated with [[Sky Watcher]], the group announced by [[Jake Barber]].[^1][^2][^3]

### Futurism

The Form D of Futurism, LLC ([[Brooklyn]], [[New York City|New York]]; Delaware file number 5743300) was signed by "Alexander Klokus," title chief executive officer, on December 19, 2016. It lists Klokus and [[Dan Sutera]] as executive officers and directors and "Johnson Bryan" and [[Michael G. Clark]] as directors (first name "Johnson," last name "Bryan," as entered on the form; the filing gives no middle name or other identifier). It reports a total offering and amount sold of 6,000,652 dollars, one investor, first sale November 22, 2016, and exemption Rule 506(b). It is the only filing under the company's [[Securities and Exchange Commission]] number.[^1] The 15 calendar days after a first sale allowed by 17 CFR 230.503(a)(1) for filing a Form D ran to December 7, 2016; the form was filed 27 days after the first sale.[^1][^4]

An archived copy of futurism.com/about dated June 16, 2017 lists Klokus as chief executive officer, with Dan Sutera as president, and lists [[Peter Diamandis]] of the [[XPRIZE Foundation]] among its "Experts."[^5] A press release of July 26, 2021 by [[Recurrent Ventures]] is titled "Recurrent Ventures Acquires Futurism from [[Singularity University]]" and does not name Klokus in the text.[^6] The speaker biography for Klokus on the [[SALT Conference]] website states that he "built and sold both the media company Futurism and the sleep and wellness business Gravity."[^7]

### Theo Rewards and portfolio companies

The Form D of Theo Rewards, Inc. (New York; filed February 3, 2020, signed by Klokus January 31, 2020) lists "Alexander Lockwood Klokus" as executive officer, director, co-chief executive officer and chief financial officer, and [[Ankur Jain]] as co-chief executive officer and secretary; 300,000 dollars offered and sold, one investor, first sale January 23, 2020.[^8]

Further Form D filings list Klokus with "Scaramucci Alexander" or "Alexander Scaramucci" ([[Alexander Scaramucci]]) as officers or directors. Tomorrow Farms Inc. (New York; filed May 24, 2022) names [[Ben Berman]] chief executive officer and signer, Scaramucci treasurer and Klokus secretary (8,500,000 dollars offered, 8,255,000 sold, 21 investors; the filing as Tomorrow Farms, PBC, of April 29, 2024, 11,849,995 offered, 4,765,635 sold, six investors). Shuttle Labs LLC (filed November 28, 2022) lists "AJ Scaramucci" and "Alex Klokus" as executive officers (25,000 dollars sold). Radial Health, Inc. (filed April 30, 2024; 8,499,990 dollars offered, 7,797,992 sold, 13 investors, first sale April 15, 2024) lists Klokus, Scaramucci and [[Elliot Cohen]] as directors and [[John Capecelatro]] as chief executive officer. Microcapital, Inc. (filed November 7, 2024; 1,535,000 dollars sold, four investors) lists Klokus and Scaramucci as directors.[^9][^10] [[Fortune]] reported on September 24, 2026 that "public filings list Alex Klokus as a third co-founder" of Radial Health.[^11]

### SALT Fund, Kyber and the adviser

The Form D of SALT Fund I, L.P. filed March 16, 2021 lists "Alexander Scaramucci" and "Alexander Klokus," both executive officers and managing members of the general partner, and was signed "Alexander Scaramucci." It reports 25,000,000 dollars offered and a first sale yet to occur; an amendment of July 6, 2021 reports a first sale on March 17, 2021, 40,000,000 dollars offered, 34,585,000 dollars sold and 91 investors.[^12] The Form D of Kyber Capital Crypto Fund I LP (signed August 23, 2022, filed August 24, 2022) was signed "Alexander Klokus, Managing Member of Kyber Capital Crypto GP I LLC"; it names SALT Fund Management, LLC as investment manager and reports a first sale yet to occur, an indefinite offering and nothing sold.[^13]

SALT Fund Management LLC (CRD 316587), an exempt reporting adviser since September 9, 2021, lists on Schedule A of the Form ADV executed March 30, 2026 "Scaramucci, Alexander, Joseph" (CRD 6960736) and "KLOKUS, ALEXANDER" (CRD 7432068), each "MANAGING MEMBER," each acquired 09/2021, each ownership code D ("50 percent but less than 75 percent"), each control person. The execution page is signed "ALEXANDER KLOKUS." The two codes are reported here as filed. The filing states private fund assets of 132,930,715 dollars.[^2] See SALT Fund Management LLC for the fund list and the related filings.

The speaker biography on the SALT conference website describes him as "Founder and Managing Partner at the SALT Fund" and "Founder & Managing Partner, Kyber Capital."[^7] The SEC investment adviser database returned no record for an individual under CRD 7432068 when queried on October 3, 2026.[^2]

### ENNEA LLC, SKYWATCHER and the Esalen gathering

The Florida foreign-LLC application for ENNEA LLC, faxed June 16, 2025 by the registered-agent firm, is signed "ALEX KLOKUS" as sole listed person with the title Member. The annual report filed April 17, 2026 is signed "ALEX KLOKUS, CEO," and the amended annual report of July 15, 2026 "ALEX KLOKUS, AUTHORIZED SIGNER."[^3] The co-existence agreement effective November 19, 2025 between ENNEA LLC and Waterkress Creative LLC carries the typed signature "Alex Klokus," title "ENNEA LLC Authorized Representative"; its Adobe Acrobat Sign audit trail records the document emailed to an ennea.io address on November 25, 2025 and signed at 12:17:02 PM GMT.[^18] See ENNEA LLC for the Delaware, USPTO and website records.

The USPTO applications for SKYWATCHER (serial 98864113, filed November 20, 2024), ENNEA (98873547, filed November 26, 2024) and PSIONIC (99091501, filed March 19, 2025) name ENNEA LLC as owner and were signed by the attorney of record, [[Robert Drolet]]; none carries an owner-signed declaration, and the owner telephone on them is the number printed on the 2016 Form D of Futurism, LLC and on the 2026 Form ADV of SALT Fund Management LLC.[^19] [[Skywatcher Technologies Corporation]] was incorporated in Delaware (file number 10021279) on November 27, 2024.[^20] [[Jason Colavito]] wrote on January 19, 2025, reporting [[Steven Greenstreet]], that Barber's website "is owned by Alex Klokus, the co-founder of the SALT conference"; [[NewsNation]]'s [[Ross Coulthart]] gave the founders as "Jake Barber, James Fowler and financier Alex Klokus."[^21] The registration record for skywatcher.ai names "Skywatcher Technologies," not Klokus, as registrant.[^22] No Form D by an issuer named Skywatcher or Ennea was found in EDGAR full-text searches on October 3, 2026.[^23]

The website ennea.io, registered January 5, 2024, announced "The Explorer's Club by Ennea. Inaugural gathering at the [[Esalen Institute]] October 14th-18th, 2024," with no named organizer.[^24] The Anomaly Archives catalogue lists [[Karl Nell]] and Klokus in a SALT iConnections session titled "Zero Doubt" Non-Human Intelligence on Earth on May 21, 2024.[^25]

On May 23, 2017 [[Masha Drokova]] wrote to [[Jeffrey Epstein]]: "You've mentioned that you need a media who's be able to write about researches you're supporting. I know the most raising media in the intersection of science and futurism. Here's https://futurism.com/ ... 25M unique good quality audience per month ... Founder https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexklokus" (EFTA01047005).[^26] On September 11 and 12, 2017 Drokova told Epstein that a Futurism editor wanted an interview on "Trump's cuts on science and your philanthropy"; Epstein answered "yes happy to do in person." The schedules sent by [[Lesley Groff]] on September 18, 2017 list at 1:30 PM "Interview w/Futurism Editor, Alex Klokus. Masha to join the interview," and at 9:50 AM that day Drokova had written to Groff "His name is Alex Klokus." The entries are scheduled items; no document records that the interview took place or what was said.[^27]

On October 2, 2017 at 2:58 PM Drokova forwarded to Epstein a "Note from Alex (Futurism founder)": "I was sitting with Bryan Johnson today, who was telling me about everything he's working on at Kernel. Much of it closely aligned with our conversations we had with Jeffrey. I feel like they would mutually [benefit] from a conversation, if Jeffrey was interested!" Epstein replied at 19:35 UTC: "always interested."[^28] The same evening, at 8:39 PM, Klokus wrote to [[Bryan Johnson]] and Drokova:

"Hello to you both, You're both already briefed, so I'll keep this short. Bryan is building Kernel, and today I spoke with Masha about the potential value of him and Jeffrey chatting. Masha has spoken with Jeffrey, and will be working with his team to hopefully coordinate time to connect before Bryan leaves on Weds. @Masha: I'll let you take it from here."

It is signed "Alex Klokus, CEO, Futurism" and survives as a quoted message in a thread of October 3, 2017 (EFTA02227011, Data Set 11), in which Johnson answered "Thank you Alex! Promoting to bcc" and added two assistants to find a time.[^29] The message is addressed to Johnson and Drokova, not to Epstein. Groff's schedules for October 6, 2017 list a Zoom interview with Johnson set for 3:00 PM, moved at Epstein's request to 5:00 PM and then, by Groff's email of 8:52 PM UTC, to 5:15 PM; Drokova wrote at 5:14 PM, "I managed to free up earlier and join Jeffrey and Bryan too." No document records that the call occurred or what was said.[^30] Searches of the library for payments among Epstein and Futurism, Klokus, Johnson or Kernel returned none; the index is OCR text and a zero is not proof of absence.[^31]
