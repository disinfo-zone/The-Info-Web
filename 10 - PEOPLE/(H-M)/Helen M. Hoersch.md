Helen M. Hoersch (died April 5, 2004, aged 83) was a daughter of [[Albert Hoersch]] and [[Helen E. Hoersch]] of 417 West Chew Street in the [[Olney, Philadelphia|Olney]] section of [[Philadelphia]], a sister of [[Raymond C. Hoersch]], and the aunt of the pornographer and fugitive [[R.C. Horsch]]. The house was searched in the [[Olney House Investigation]] of 2026.[^1][^2][^3]

### Life

She was born in Philadelphia and lived there all her life. She graduated from the [[University of Pennsylvania]] and worked for many years as a senior editor at the [[American Society for Testing and Materials]], retiring at 79. She did not marry. Her father's death notice of 1972 gives the family address as 417 West Chew Street, and her brother Raymond's of 1991 lists her among the survivors as "Helen M., of Phila."[^1][^2][^4]

### Death and the House

She died on April 5, 2004, "at the home of her nephew." Her death notice names as survivors her nephew Ray Horsch and three nieces, and her funeral was arranged by the Cremation Society of Pennsylvania.[^1] In September 2026 police said they believed three of the five urns found in the Chew Avenue house held the ashes of R.C. Horsch, his sister, and a friend; one 2026 *Inquirer* account calls Helen M. Hoersch his sister.[^3][^5] *The Philadelphia Inquirer* reported in 2026 that R.C. Horsch acquired the Chew Avenue house after her death and in 2007 used a power of attorney to transfer it to his son, [[Eugene Horsch]], for one dollar; one of its accounts calls her his sister. The city's deed index records a deed executed on October 1, 2007, and recorded on October 26, 2007, with "Hoersch Helen M" as grantor and Eugene A. Horsch as grantee.[^3][^6][^7]
