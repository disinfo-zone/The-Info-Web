Clara Hoersch, born Clara Hagerty, was the wife of the engineer and former glider pilot [[Raymond C. Hoersch]] and the mother of the [[Philadelphia]] pornographer, counterfeiter, and fugitive [[R.C. Horsch]]. She was a daughter of [[Eugene D. Hagerty]] and [[Cecilia T. Hagerty]] of [[Stroudsburg, Pennsylvania|Stroudsburg]] in the [[Pocono Mountains|Poconos]], where her son was born in 1943.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

### Family

In January 1955 a [[Bucks County, Pennsylvania|Bucks County]] social column reported that Mr. and Mrs. Raymond Hoersch had been honored at a New Year's Eve party in [[Paoli, Pennsylvania|Paoli]] for their thirteenth wedding anniversary, and that her brother and sister-in-law, Mr. and Mrs. Elwood Hagerty of [[Reading, Pennsylvania|Reading]], had visited them.[^1] Her son said that his mother was a housewife during his early childhood on a farm near Stroudsburg.[^5] In September 1969 she and her husband, then of [[Doylestown, Pennsylvania|Doylestown]], attended her parents' fiftieth wedding anniversary in Stroudsburg with their daughter Helen.[^6]

In September 1971 her husband bought property in [[Chalfont, Pennsylvania|Chalfont]], and the family house at 523 North Main Street was the address their son gave in his 1972 suit against the Army and the house where, according to officials, he had been living when the [[United States Secret Service]] arrested him for counterfeiting in December 1974.[^7][^8] Her mother's obituary of October 1991 lists her among the survivors as "Clara Hoersch of Chalfont, Bucks County," with her brothers Elwood, of [[Wyomissing Hills, Pennsylvania|Wyomissing Hills]], and Olen, Ottmar, and Carl, of Stroudsburg.[^2]
