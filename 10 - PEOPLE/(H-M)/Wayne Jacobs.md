Wayne A. Jacobs is the special agent in charge of the Philadelphia Field Office of the [[Federal Bureau of Investigation]], named by Director [[Christopher Wray]]. He appeared with Commissioner [[Kevin Bethel]] and Deputy Commissioner [[Frank Vanore]] of the [[Philadelphia Police Department]] at the briefing of September 16, 2026, on the [[Olney House Investigation]], and said that further search warrants were planned for the house on Chew Avenue.[^1][^2]

### Career

Jacobs was a trading systems analyst before joining the FBI as a special agent in 2003. He was assigned to the New York Field Office, where he worked on government and financial fraud and later violent crime and gangs. He became a supervisory special agent in the Criminal Investigative Division at headquarters in 2014, a violent gangs task force supervisor in [[Los Angeles]] in 2016, and assistant special agent in charge of the Mission Support Branch in [[Newark, New Jersey|Newark]] in 2018. In 2020 he was promoted to section chief in the [[FBI Counterintelligence Division|Counterintelligence Division]] at headquarters, and in 2021 he became special agent in charge of the Criminal and Cyber Division of the Washington Field Office. He holds a bachelor's degree from the [[University of Delaware]] and a master's degree from [[Columbia University]].[^1]
