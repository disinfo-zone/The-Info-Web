[[David F. Levi]] was [[United States Attorney|United States Attorney]] for the [[Eastern District of California]] from 1986 to 1990, after serving as an assistant United States attorney in the same district from 1983 to 1986, and became a federal district judge in 1990.[^1] He was named in a Writ of Mandamus filed by [[Ben Wagner]] for refusing to investigate corruption in Mariposa County.[^2]
