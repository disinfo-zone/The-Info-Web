# Business & Finance

Executives, financiers, bankers and business families whose dealings with intelligence agencies, governments or the vault's scandals are documented here without their holding office themselves.

```dataview
TABLE WITHOUT ID
  file.link AS Name,
  summary AS Description
FROM "10 - PEOPLE"
WHERE category = "Business & Finance"
SORT file.name ASC
```
