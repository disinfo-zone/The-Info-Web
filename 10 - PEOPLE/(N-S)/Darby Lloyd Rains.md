Darby Lloyd Rains, born Barbara in 1947 ("not 1948 as it says some places on the internet," she said), is a former adult film actress who was among the early group of New York performers of the 1970s with [[Marc Stevens]], [[Tina Russell]], [[Georgina Spelvin]] and [[Jamie Gillis]]; she played the lead in *Naked Came the Stranger* (1975).[^1][^2]

### Early Life

She grew up in [[Yorktown Heights, New York|Yorktown Heights]] in [[Westchester County, New York|Westchester County]]. Her father, from a musical family of the Pacific Northwest, had roles in Broadway productions of the 1950s including *[[Brigadoon]]* and *[[The Pajama Game]]*, toured with a male quartet, the Revelers, and later taught music at a local high school. With a friend she formed a singing duo, Cranberry, which recorded for [[Liberty Records]] with Joe Negroni of Frankie Lymon and the Teenagers as producer; the record never had a real release.[^1] Her early films included *Sex U.S.A.* and *Dark Dreams* (1971).[^1] In February 1973 she appeared in *[[The Erotic Memoirs of a Male Chauvinist Pig]]*, shot in [[Philadelphia]] by [[R.C. Horsch]].[^3][^4]

### Elizabeth Arrest

In 1974, she said, Stevens persuaded her to make a personal appearance at a new theater in [[Elizabeth, New Jersey]], the Liberty, which was showing *Lady on a Couch*; police arrested her, Stevens and a man named Allen under a local ordinance against exhibiting adult films. "I told the cops, 'We're not exhibiting anything!'" she said. The authorities, she said, released them on condition that they sign a document absolving the police of blame. She left New York in the late 1970s.[^2]
