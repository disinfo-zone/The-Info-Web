### Identity

Bustle reported on August 1, 2017 that Alexander Scaramucci is "the oldest child of the former [[White House]] communications director" [[Anthony Scaramucci]], "also known as AJ according to The Daily Mail," then 24.[^1] [[Fortune]] reported on September 24, 2026 that he is the son of "a chemical engineer" and "serial entrepreneur Anthony Scaramucci".[^2]

The [[Financial Industry Regulatory Authority]] and [[Securities and Exchange Commission]] individual record (CRD 6960736, "Alexander Joseph Scaramucci") lists the examinations Series 7 (July 10, 2018), Series 63 (August 9, 2018) and the Securities Industry Essentials exam (September 13, 2018), no current or previous employments and no disclosures.[^3]

### Diamandis

Bustle quoted his LinkedIn page in 2017: after [[Tufts University]] and more than a year at [[Google]] he was an entrepreneur-in-residence, "I work with Peter Diamandis across all of his ventures: XPRIZE, Human Longevity Inc., Planetary Resources, Bold Capital Partners, Singularity University, Abundance 360."[^1] In the Fortune interview he said he read [[Peter Diamandis]]'s book "Abundance" in April 2012, entered through Chris Lewicki, then chief executive of [[Planetary Resources]], and "by 2015" was an entrepreneur-in-residence under Diamandis, "building the CAR-T cell therapy startup [[Celularity|Cellularity]] and the education venture Abundance Group." Diamandis told Fortune he had known him "for the better part of 15 years," that he was in Diamandis's "Strike Force" program, and that he was "happy to be a limited partner in Solari Capital"; the article lists Diamandis among Solari's backers.[^2] The [[SALT Conference]] speaker page for AJ Scaramucci (tagged [[Las Vegas]] 2019, New York 2022, Asia 2022 and New York 2023) gives a similar biography; Diamandis's page carries the tag SALT Las Vegas 2019.[^4]

### SALT Fund Management and Kyber

SALT Fund Management LLC (CRD 316587, SEC file 802-122469; [[Palm Beach, Florida|Palm Beach]] Gardens, Florida) has reported as an exempt reporting adviser since September 9, 2021. Schedule A of its annual amendment executed March 30, 2026 lists "Scaramucci, Alexander, Joseph" and "KLOKUS, ALEXANDER" ([[Alex Klokus]], CRD 7432068), each "MANAGING MEMBER," each acquired September 2021, each with ownership code D (50 percent but less than 75 percent) and control person status; the form was signed by Klokus. Item 2.B reports private fund assets of $132,930,715. The funds listed include Kyber Capital Crypto Fund I ($54,529,900 gross assets, 14 owners), SALT Fund I ($38,519,622, 106 owners), a gene therapy fund ($16,432,917), a longevity series ($14,044,756), a food series ($7,180,412) and a mental health series ($2,223,108).[^5]

[[SALT Fund I, L.P.]] filed a Form D on March 16, 2021, signed "/s/ Alexander Scaramucci," naming Scaramucci and Klokus as executive officers and offering $25,000,000 with the first sale yet to occur; an amendment of July 6, 2021 reports a first sale on March 17, 2021, $40,000,000 offered, $34,585,000 sold and 91 investors.[^6] The series funds (food, longevity, gene therapy, mental health), filed between November 2021 and June 2022 under general partner entities including SALT Equity Partners I LLC, do not name either man as a related person.[^6]

Kyber Capital Crypto Fund I LP filed a Form D signed August 23, 2022 (EDGAR August 24, 2022), naming Kyber Capital Crypto GP I LLC as general partner, SALT Fund Management LLC as investment manager, and Klokus and "Alexander Joseph Scaramucci" as managing members; it reports the first sale as "yet to occur," an "Indefinite" offering of more than one year and $0 sold. EDGAR lists no later Form D for the fund.[^7] The $54,529,900 and 14 owners are the figures reported on the March 2026 Form ADV.[^5]

### Other issuers

Form D filings name him alongside Klokus as a director or officer of [[Tomorrow Farms Inc.|Tomorrow Farms]] Inc. (May 24, 2022: Ben Berman chief executive, Scaramucci treasurer, Klokus secretary; $8,255,000 sold to 21 investors; a second filing for Tomorrow Farms, PBC, April 29, 2024, $4,765,635 sold to 6 investors), Shuttle Labs LLC (November 28, 2022, $25,000 sold), [[Radial Health, Inc.|Radial Health]] Inc. (April 30, 2024, directors Klokus, Scaramucci and Elliot Cohen; $7,797,992 sold to 13 investors) and Microcapital Inc. (November 7, 2024, $1,535,000 sold to 4 investors).[^8] Fortune reports that "public filings list Alex Klokus as a third co-founder" of Radial Health.[^2]

[[Solari Capital]] funds (Fund III, ABTC I and II, General Medicine I, Northwood Space I, Treasure Trove I and Radial Health I, filed December 2024 to May 2026) name only Alexander Scaramucci as managing member of the general partner. Fund III offered $75,000,000 and reported $42,900,000 sold to 39 investors (D/A December 19, 2025), and reports a finder, CF Advisory Pte. Ltd, paid on committed capital.[^9] Fortune reported that Solari has deployed about $350 million and describes SALT as "the investor conference and media business Scaramucci co-chairs."[^2]

### FTX bankruptcy documents

The creditor matrix filed January 25, 2023 in the bankruptcy of [[FTX|FTX Trading Ltd.]] (Bankr. D. Del. 22-11068, Doc. 574, page 102 of 116) lists creditor "THE SALT FUND" with contact lines "AJ SCARAMUCCI," "ALEX KLOKUS" and a third individual, and a second row "THE SALT FUND / AJ SCARAMUCCI"; no amount or claim type is stated. "SALT Fund Management, LLC" appears on the debtors' updated Interested Parties list of May 10, 2024 (Doc. 14607-1, page 7), and "The Salt Fund" with email addresses at the thesaltfund.com domain appears on the Notice Parties Service List served June 13, 2024 with the order approving the sale of FTX's Anthropic shares (Doc. 18381, Exhibit B).[^10] [[SkyBridge Capital]], the firm of Anthony Scaramucci, has a separate row in the matrix.[^10]
