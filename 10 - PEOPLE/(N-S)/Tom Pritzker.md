Thomas J. Pritzker (born June 6, 1950) is an American billionaire businessman, the executive chairman of [[Hyatt Hotels Corporation]] for more than twenty years until February 17, 2026, and the chairman of the broader [[Pritzker Organization|Pritzker family]] business empire. The Pritzker family's holdings span hotels (Hyatt), industrial manufacturing ([[Marmon Group|Marmon Group/Trinity Industries]]), finance, and the philanthropic [[Pritzker Foundation|Pritzker Foundation]], and the family's cumulative wealth places it among the wealthiest American business dynasties. Pritzker's correspondence with [[Jeffrey Epstein]] in 2011, documented in the [[Epstein Files Transparency Act|EFTA]] releases as document [[EFTA02407727|EFTA02407727]], used the phrase "secret society," establishing a second documented "secret society" channel through Epstein independent of the [[Peter Thiel]] correspondence. Epstein's calendar scheduled 28 meetings with Pritzker between 2013 and 2018, and after the 2025 and 2026 releases of their correspondence Pritzker retired from Hyatt, saying he had "exercised terrible judgment."[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]

### The Pritzker Family and the Hyatt Empire

The [[Pritzker family|Pritzker family]] built its wealth across the twentieth century through a diversified holding structure anchored by Hyatt (founded by Pritzker's father [[Donald Pritzker|Donald]] and uncle [[Jay Pritzker|Jay]] in 1957), the Marmon Group industrial conglomerate, the [[Superior Bank|Superior Bank]] (which failed in 2001 in an early instance of the securitization-driven banking collapses), and a range of venture and private-equity holdings. Thomas Pritzker succeeded his father and uncle as the principal executive of the family's business operations and took Hyatt public in 2009.[^1]

The Pritzker family's philanthropic arm, the Pritzker Foundation, has funded the [[Pritzker School of Medicine|Pritzker School of Medicine]] at the [[University of Chicago]], the [[Pritzker Military Museum & Library|Pritzker Military Museum & Library]], and a range of cultural and educational institutions in [[Chicago]] and nationally. [[J.B. Pritzker|J.B. Pritzker]], Thomas's cousin, is the current [[Governor of Illinois|Governor of Illinois]] and a prominent [[Democratic Party|Democratic]] political figure. The family's political and philanthropic footprint spans both parties and multiple institutional sectors.[^1]

### The Epstein Correspondence and the "Secret Society" Email

The EFTA releases include document EFTA02407727, a 2011 correspondence between Pritzker and Jeffrey Epstein in which Pritzker used the phrase "secret society." Epstein's reply, per the document, was "Remind me next month." The document is indexed in the [[Epstein Files|Epstein file]] aggregators and cited in the secondary reporting on the Epstein-Pritzker relationship. The correspondence is dated 2011, three years after Epstein's 2008 federal [[Epstein non-prosecution agreement|non-prosecution agreement]].[^2][^3]

The Pritzker "secret society" email runs parallel to the Thiel-Epstein "secret society idea" correspondence (documented in [[EFTA01738574|EFTA01738574]], dated February 2016). The two are independent channels: the Thiel correspondence records Epstein reporting that Thiel "LOVED the secret socieity [sic] idea," and the Pritzker correspondence records Pritzker himself using the phrase. Whether the two channels refer to the same "secret society" concept or to separate projects is not established in the primary documents. The 2011 Pritzker email predates the 2016 Thiel email by five years.[^2][^6]

### The Pritzker-Epstein Relationship

The *[[The Wall Street Journal|Wall Street Journal]]* reported in 2023, on the basis of the Epstein calendars, that Pritzker was among the business figures who met with Epstein repeatedly in the post-2008-conviction period. Pritzker's meetings with Epstein included discussions that Pritzker's representatives framed as relating to philanthropic and business matters. The *Wall Street Journal* reporting placed Pritzker alongside [[William Burns]], [[Noam Chomsky]], and the former Goldman Sachs lawyer [[Kathryn Ruemmler|Kathryn Ruemmler]] in the group of establishment figures whose Epstein meetings were documented in the calendars.[^3]

The Pritzker-Epstein relationship is the parallel establishment channel to the Thiel-Epstein relationship. Both channels run through Epstein in the post-2008-conviction period, both involve "secret society" language, and both connect Epstein to principals of major business and institutional networks (the Pritzker hotel-and-finance empire and the Thiel technology-and-defense empire respectively). The question of whether the two channels intersect, beyond the shared Epstein intermediary, is not documented in the primary sources currently available.[^2][^3][^6]

### The 2025 Email Release

The [[House Committee on Oversight and Government Reform|House Oversight Committee]]'s November 2025 release of documents from Epstein's estate showed that Epstein's calendar scheduled Pritzker for 28 meetings between 2013 and 2018.[^4] The two exchanged friendly emails on at least five occasions, including one about "the Chicago way of politics."[^7] In August 2016 Pritzker wrote, "Looking for a time to catch up in NY," and Epstein replied, "I'm in santa fe for the next two weeks come visit."[^4]

Epstein offered Pritzker's time to others. Writing to a third party whom he offered to introduce to Pritzker, he described Pritzker as "like family." He asked another contact whether they had "input or questions" for an event Pritzker was chairing in [[China]], and told a correspondent that "tom has GREAT relations in China."[^4] The [[Department of Justice|Justice Department]]'s early 2026 releases added further emails between the two arranging dinners and invitations to events.[^5]

### CSIS, the Aspen Strategy Group, and Churkin

In November 2015 Pritzker became chairman of the board of trustees of the [[Center for Strategic and International Studies]] (CSIS) in Washington, succeeding the former senator [[Sam Nunn]], after eight years on its board and executive committee. He was also a member of the [[Aspen Strategy Group]].[^8]

On the morning of August 8, 2016, Epstein wrote to Pritzker, "glenn?" Pritzker answered that he was "working on a date" and had "Just finished presentation on leadership to grand pooh baahs of Aspen Strategy Group. Take a look at members it will make you laugh." Epstein replied, "who attends." That evening Pritzker wrote: "You have to be a bit of a wonk to know some of the names, but Nye, Scowcroft and Nick Burns run it (Scowcroft being replaced by Condi). Members include Patraeus, Albright, Slaughter, Donalon (Obama NSA), Hadley (Bush NSA), Sullivan (NSA Hillary), Bill Burns (State #2), Kurt Campbell, Hoss Carwright (#2 Jt Chiefs), Harman, Ignatius, Sanger, Nunn, Reed, Sawers ([[MI6]]), Sherman (iran deal), Work (current #2 DoD). It seems to now be taking on an aura. Looking for a time to come catch up in NY."[^9] The names correspond to the co-chairs [[Joseph Nye]], [[Brent Scowcroft]] and [[Nicholas Burns]], Scowcroft's successor [[Condoleezza Rice]], and the members [[David Petraeus]], [[Madeleine Albright]], [[Anne-Marie Slaughter]], [[Tom Donilon]], [[Stephen Hadley]], [[Jake Sullivan]], William Burns, [[Kurt Campbell]], [[James Cartwright]], [[Jane Harman]], [[David Ignatius]], [[David Sanger]], Nunn, [[Jack Reed (senator)|Jack Reed]], [[John Sawers]], [[Wendy Sherman]] and [[Robert Work]].

Four weeks later, on September 6, 2016, Epstein wrote by iMessage to [[Vitaly Churkin]], Russia's ambassador to the [[United Nations]]: "My very close friend Tom Pritzker . Hyatt hotels and chairman of CSI is in town . Didn't know if you had any interest." Churkin replied, "Could do tomorrow 6pm," and thanked Epstein "for time with max," his son Maxim. The next morning Epstein wrote, "Re Pritzker -my schedule has gotten too crowded maybe 18 th or 19th if you are in town." Churkin answered, "18 might work," and Epstein added, "great. also thorbjorn jagland will be staying with me," a reference to [[Thorbjørn Jagland]], then secretary general of the [[Council of Europe]].[^10] Eight days earlier, on August 29, 2016, Churkin had lunched at Epstein's house with [[Ehud Barak]] and [[Tom Barrack]].[^11]

### Retirement From Hyatt

On February 17, 2026, Pritzker, then 75, retired as executive chairman of Hyatt with immediate effect and said he would not stand for re-election to its board. In a prepared statement he said he deeply regretted his association with Epstein and [[Ghislaine Maxwell]]: "I exercised terrible judgment in maintaining contact with them, and there is no excuse for failing to distance myself sooner. I condemn the actions and the harm caused by Epstein and Maxwell and I feel deep sorrow for the pain they inflicted on their victims."[^5] Hyatt's chief executive, [[Mark Hoplamazian]], succeeded him as chairman. His retirement came days after Kathryn Ruemmler announced her resignation as general counsel of [[Goldman Sachs]] and after [[DP World]] replaced its chairman over his Epstein ties.[^5] Pritzker has not been accused of any crime, and no investigation of him has been reported.
