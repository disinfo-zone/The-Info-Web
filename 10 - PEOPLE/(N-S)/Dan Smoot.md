Dan Smoot was the broadcasting name of Howard D. Smoot, a special agent of the [[Federal Bureau of Investigation|FBI]] from March 23, 1942, to June 15, 1951, and afterward the commentator of [[Facts Forum]], the radio and television foundation of [[H.L. Hunt]], and the publisher of The [[The Dan Smoot Report|Dan Smoot Report]].[^1]

### Facts Forum

The masthead of [[Facts Forum News]] for June 1955 lists Smoot as commentator and [[Medford Evans]] as editor. On July 11, 1955, the former bureau officials [[Stanley Tracy]] and [[D. Milton Ladd|D.M. Ladd]] passed word to headquarters that Hunt "is withdrawing his support of Facts Forum," that he "was despondent over the recent death of his wife and that there was considerable dissension among the office staff," and that "former Special Agent Daniel Smoot is planning to leave the Facts Forum set up as of August 1, 1955." Smoot started his own newsletter and broadcast. A bureau memorandum of February 26, 1964, listed him with [[Robert E. Lee (FCC)|Robert E. Lee]] of the [[Federal Communications Commission]] and [[Gene Scudder|Milton Eugene Scudder]] as "former Agents associated with H.L. Hunt's right-wing groups."[^2][^3]

### The Bureau's Two Accounts

Through the 1960s members of the public wrote to the FBI about Smoot, most of them after hearing his commentary on the [[John Birch Society]]. The form reply stated that he had "voluntarily submitted his resignation" for personal reasons, that he had had no access to bureau files since, and that his views "do not represent this Bureau in any manner." An internal note in the same file reads: "Howard D. Smoot, former SA, resigned following censure, probation and transfer due to several unfounded charges made by him against his SAC."[^1]
