Ian John Shearer (died June 1, 2021) was a [[New Zealand]] National Party politician who represented Hamilton East from 1975 to 1984. He was appointed Minister for the Environment and Minister of Science and Technology on February 12, 1981, taking the science portfolio from [[Bill Birch]], and Minister of Broadcasting on December 11, 1981. He resigned all three offices on July 26, 1984, after National's election defeat.[^1][^2][^3] The House of Representatives recorded his death on June 3, 2021.[^4]

### The Temuka Plant

In April 1982, on the same visit to Canterbury on which he opened the Dairy Science and Technology Conference in [[Christchurch]], Shearer opened "the whey-to-alcohol factory of Chemical Technology, Ltd, at Temuka," the plant of [[Chemical Technology Limited]], and said it would turn what twenty years earlier had been "poured down the throat of a pig" into alcohol and proteins.[^5] The company's American director, Dr Stanley Stokowski, was the fugitive [[R.C. Horsch]], who had entered the country in 1980 under a stolen identity.
