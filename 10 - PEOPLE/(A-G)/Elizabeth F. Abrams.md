Elizabeth F. Abrams has served as an Assistant United States Attorney in the Eastern District of Pennsylvania. She prosecuted *United States v. Horsch*, No. 2:09-cr-00794, the case against [[R.C. Horsch]] for growing marijuana and possessing firearms at his house in [[New Britain Township, Pennsylvania|New Britain Township]].[^1]

### United States v. Horsch

The indictment was filed on December 15, 2009, before Judge [[Richard Barclay Surrick]], with [[Jerome Brown]] appearing for the defense. Abrams filed an information under 21 U.S.C. section 851 on December 23, 2010, giving notice of Horsch's prior drug convictions so as to enhance his sentence. He pleaded guilty on January 10, 2011, to possessing 100 or more marijuana plants with intent to distribute, possessing a short-barreled shotgun in furtherance of drug trafficking, and possessing a firearm as a convicted felon, and was sentenced in 2012 to 54 months; his house on Airy Avenue was forfeited.[^1]

### Other Cases

In December 2014, under United States Attorney [[Zane David Memeger]], she was named as the prosecutor of a Philadelphia man charged with possessing a firearm as a convicted felon, in a case investigated by the [[Federal Bureau of Investigation|FBI]] and the [[Philadelphia Police Department]].[^2]
