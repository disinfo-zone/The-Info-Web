Robert Garcia (born December 2, 1977) is a Democratic member of the [[United States House of Representatives]] from the 42nd district of [[California]], in office since January 3, 2023, and the ranking member of the [[House Committee on Oversight and Government Reform]]. He was previously mayor of [[Long Beach, California|Long Beach]]. As ranking member he released correspondence from the estate of [[Jeffrey Epstein]] concerning [[Donald Trump]], joined the ranking member of the [[House Committee on the Judiciary]], [[Jamie Raskin]], in inquiring into [[Ghislaine Maxwell]]'s treatment at [[Federal Prison Camp Bryan]], and supported the 2026 subpoena of Attorney General [[Pam Bondi]].[^1][^2][^3]

### The Estate Productions

The committee's chairman, [[James Comer]], copied Garcia as ranking member on his August 25, 2025 subpoena to Epstein's estate. On November 12, 2025 the committee's Democratic staff released three emails from the estate's latest production. In a 2011 email to Maxwell, Epstein wrote: "that dog that hasn't barked is trump.. [Victim] spent hours at my house with him ,, he has never once been mentioned. Police chief. etc. im 75 % there." In a 2019 email to the writer [[Michael Wolff]] he wrote, "Of course he knew about the girls as he asked ghislaine to stop." A 2015 exchange between Epstein and Wolff concerned how to "craft an answer" for a Trump interview on [[CNN]]. Garcia said: "The more Donald Trump tries to cover up the [[Epstein Files|Epstein files]], the more we uncover. These latest emails and correspondence raise glaring questions about what else the [[White House]] is hiding and the nature of the relationship between Epstein and the President. The [[Department of Justice]] must fully release the Epstein files to the public immediately."[^4][^5] Comer, releasing the estate's first production on September 8, 2025, had said that "President Trump is not accused of any wrongdoing."[^6]

### FPC Bryan

On January 23, 2026 Garcia and Raskin wrote to Bondi and to Acting Inspector General Don R. Berthiaume of the [[Department of Justice Office of the Inspector General]], citing "over a dozen whistleblowers" who reported that "Ms. Maxwell has been permitted to use a laptop, unsupervised, while at Bryan," that she had "access to staff-only areas to watch CNN by herself," that "the Warden sends out Ms. Maxwell's mail under the Warden's own name," and that the warden "fired those who made protected disclosures to Congress." They asked that the department make "Warden Tanisha Hall available for a transcribed interview" and that the inspector general "initiate an investigation into the reported staff misconduct."[^7]

### The Bondi Subpoena

On March 4, 2026 the committee voted 24 to 19, on a motion by the Republican [[Nancy Mace]] supported by all Democrats present and four other Republicans, to subpoena Bondi. Garcia said: "For months, Attorney General Bondi has been instrumental in orchestrating the White House's cover-up of the Epstein files," and, "The American people deserve transparency, survivors deserve justice, and we are demanding answers."[^8]
