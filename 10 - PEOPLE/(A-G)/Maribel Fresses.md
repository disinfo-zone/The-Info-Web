Maribel "Bella" Fresses, who grew up in [[Holland, Pennsylvania|Holland]], [[Bucks County, Pennsylvania|Bucks County]], was reported missing in 2018 at 27. On August 12, 2026, Philadelphia police identified her, with [[Gabrielle Amarando]], as one of two women shown alive and then apparently dead in video and photographs recovered from the house of [[R.C. Horsch]] and [[Eugene Horsch]] on West Chew Avenue in [[Olney, Philadelphia|Olney]], in the [[Olney House Investigation]]. Her body has not been found.[^1][^2]

She began using drugs in high school, according to her father, Ricardo Fresses, who said she called him every day until one day she stopped. He last saw her on June 19, 2018, when they met for a slice of pizza in [[Kensington, Philadelphia|Kensington]] and he gave her 50 dollars, and he reported her missing shortly afterward; the *[[The Philadelphia Inquirer|Philadelphia Inquirer]]* also gives February 2018 as the date of the report. "As the years went by I knew she was gone," he said. "She was a beautiful girl, a loving girl, always daddy's girl." Her brother, Dawson Fresses, said: "It's been eight years since I've seen her. Hearing that, I just couldn't believe it. I had gotten to the point where I thought I might never find out what happened to her."[^1][^2]
