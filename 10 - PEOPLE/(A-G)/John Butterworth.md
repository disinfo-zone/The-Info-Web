John R. Butterworth was a [[Philadelphia]] photographer and cameraman and a collaborator of the filmmaker Ray Hoersch, later known as [[R.C. Horsch]], with whom he shot the pornographic feature *[[The Erotic Memoirs of a Male Chauvinist Pig]]* in February 1973. Horsch called him "a close friend at the time" and described him as a news cameraman for [[KYW-TV]]; no station record of his employment has been located.[^1][^2]

### With Horsch

In April 1971 the *Philadelphia Daily News* described the Renaissance Studio, a loft at 752 South 2nd Street near Kenilworth Street where Horsch was shooting "his last scene, two reels worth" with a model and a live snake; visitors entered "through the rear past John Butterworth's still-crated photography equipment."[^3] Two years later he and Horsch made *The Erotic Memoirs of a Male Chauvinist Pig* with [[Georgina Spelvin]], [[Tina Russell]], [[Darby Lloyd Rains]], and [[Helen Madigan]]; Horsch said the money came mostly from the bar owner [[Salvatore La Russa]].[^1][^2]

In September 1991 the *[[The Philadelphia Inquirer|Philadelphia Inquirer]]* listed the registration of the fictitious name Butterworth's Hickory Pit, a barbecue restaurant in [[North Wales, Pennsylvania|North Wales]], with Raymond C. Horsch and John R. Butterworth as the registrants, at the North Wales train station. Horsch listed the restaurant as a trade name in his Chapter 13 bankruptcy petition of 1993.[^4][^5]
