Mark Grobmyer is a lawyer with the firm of [[Arnold Grobmyer & Haley]]. He is a godson of [[Jackson Stephens]] and often did legal work for [[Stephens Inc.]][^1]

### Clinton and Gore

Grobmyer was a law student at the [[University of Arkansas]] while [[Bill Clinton]] taught there. A longtime friend of [[Al Gore|Albert Gore]] and [[Tipper Gore]], he introduced Clinton to Gore in 1988. During the 1992 presidential election he was a member of the Clinton-Gore Arkansas Finance Council. He and his wife, Libby, were profiled in a 1996 report by the [[Center for Public Integrity]] on overnight guests of the Clinton [[White House]]; he did not respond to the Center's inquiries.[^1]
