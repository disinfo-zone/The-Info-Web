Naina Bajekal is a journalist and the managing director for communications of Coefficient Giving, the name taken in November 2025 by [[Open Philanthropy]], the grantmaker of [[Dustin Moskovitz]] and [[Cari Tuna]]. She joined in July 2024.[^1]

### Journalism

Bajekal holds a B.A. in English literature and modern languages from the [[University of Oxford]]. She began as a reporter in the [[London]] bureau of *[[Time]]*, held editing roles at *[[Newsweek]]*, [[Magnum Photos]] and the [[Fuller Project]], and returned to *Time* in 2018, where over six years she was international editor and executive editor, edited more than 30 cover stories and launched Women of the Year and the TIME100 AI list.[^1]

### Coefficient Giving

In December 2025 *[[Semafor]]* reported that *Time* had hosted four fellows of the [[Tarbell Center for AI Journalism]], most of whose funding came from Coefficient Giving. Bajekal said Coefficient had "no involvement in coverage decisions" and that AI held "enormous potential to accelerate science and fuel economic growth."[^2]
