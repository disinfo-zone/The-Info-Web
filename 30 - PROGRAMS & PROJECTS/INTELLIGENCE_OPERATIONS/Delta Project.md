The Delta Project was the operational name for [[John David Norman]]'s child trafficking and prostitution network, also referred to as the [[Odyssey Network]]. Norman came to the Chicago area in mid-1973 from [[Dallas, Texas|Dallas]], where the Odyssey Foundation had operated, and the Chicago Tribune reported that the Delta Project began in [[Cook County Jail]] in 1976 and was run from a Chicago post office box afterward.[^1] The Delta Project operated through a system of "sponsors" connected via coded communications in the [[Hermes]] newsletter and front organizations including the [[Odyssey Foundation]] and [[Educational Foundation for Youth]]. The writer S. Dovey wrote that an FBI memo from February 1977 confirmed the Delta Project's connection to [[Troop 137]] in New Orleans and noted that Norman's conviction in December 1976 had ended an ongoing investigation that had continued under [[Phillip Paske]]. The Delta Project was linked to [[Francis Shelden]]'s [[North Fox Island]] operation, [[Father Bud's Boys Farm]] in Tennessee, and [[John Wayne Gacy]]'s network in Chicago through shared participants.[^2]

An October 19, 1976, memorandum of the FBI Chicago office (file 166-2548) records information from an investigator for the Cook County state's attorney's office that Norman, John Shanks and Phillip Paske ran "The Delta Project" as a business transporting young males interstate to paying customers, some of whom may be federal employees.[^3]
