[[Project 8200]] is a program related to UFO/UAP (Unidentified Aerial Phenomena) bases and activities. It is mentioned in the context of [[Frederick Atwater|Skip Atwater]]'s publications, specifically his 2024 book *Project 8200: UFO/UAP Bases and Activities: The original Remote Viewing Transcripts* (ISBN-10 3911151047). The book's own description is of UAP bases identified through remote viewing. The number in the title is shared with [[Unit 8200]], the Israeli signals-intelligence unit.[^1]
