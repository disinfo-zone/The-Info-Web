### Fort Detrick and Deep Creek Lake

[[Frank Olson]], an Army bacteriologist and [[Central Intelligence Agency|CIA]] contractor, worked on biological weapons at [[Fort Detrick]] in Frederick. On November 18 and 19, 1953, [[Sidney Gottlieb]] convened a retreat of about ten [[Office of Technical Service|Technical Services Staff]] officers and contractors at a cabin at Deep Creek Lake in western Maryland, where Olson was among those given [[LSD]] without warning; he died in New York nine days later.[^1][^2] The Army gave LSD to soldiers at [[Edgewood Arsenal]] in a parallel program.[^1] Garland Williams of the [[Federal Bureau of Narcotics]] helped establish the [[Office of Strategic Services]] training schools in Maryland and Virginia.[^3] [[Frank Wisner]], who had directed the CIA's Office of Policy Coordination, retired to his farm in Maryland under psychiatric care and shot himself on October 29, 1965.[^11]

### Remote Viewing

The Army's remote-viewing program began at [[Fort Meade]] in 1977 under the name Gondola Wish, and continued as Grill Flame, [[Center Lane]] and [[Sun Streak]] before the [[Stargate Project|Star Gate]] name.[^4] At [[Aberdeen Proving Ground]], [[John Kramar]] was chief of the Ground Warfare Division of the [[Army Materiel Systems Analysis Activity]], which evaluated Grill Flame in 1979, and the Aberdeen physicist [[Evan Harris Walker]] was named principal investigator of a 1978 Army request to fund a remote-viewing machine.[^5][^6] [[Eldon Byrd]] studied the effects of electromagnetic fields on living systems at the Naval Surface Weapons Center at White Oak.[^7]

### Accelerationism and the Zizians

The federal case against members of [[The Base]], 8:20-cr-00033, was brought in the District of Maryland; William Garfield Bilbrough IV of Denton pleaded guilty in December 2020 to conspiring to transport and harbor the Canadian reservist [[Patrik Jordan Mathews]], whom he had driven east from Michigan after August 30, 2019.[^8] In April 2025 the Maryland Coordination and Analysis Center, the state's fusion center, published an advisory describing The Base's founder, [[Rinaldo Nazzaro]], as having "for years garnered suspicions of being a Russian intelligence asset."[^9] On February 16, 2025, [[Jack LaSota]] of [[The Zizians]] was arrested with [[Michelle Zajko]] and Daniel Blank near Frostburg in Allegany County, where they were living in two box trucks, and prosecutors moved to try the group's members together in Maryland.[^10]
