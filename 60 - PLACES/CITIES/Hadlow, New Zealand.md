Hadlow is a rural settlement near [[Timaru]] in South Canterbury, [[New Zealand]].

### The Stokowski Affair

The American known as Dr Stanley Stokowski, a director of the ethanol plant of [[Chemical Technology Limited]] at Clandeboye, lived at Hadlow until mid-March 1984, when he left the country on a stolen passport.[^1] In the weeks that followed, police searched properties at Clandeboye and Hadlow.[^2] The man was the fugitive [[R.C. Horsch]].
