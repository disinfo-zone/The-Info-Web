### BNL Atlanta

Between 1985 and 1989 the Atlanta branch of the [[Banca Nazionale del Lavoro]], managed by [[Christopher Drogoul]], extended more than four billion dollars in unreported loans and credits to Iraq, part of them guaranteed by the [[Commodity Credit Corporation Iraq Program|Commodity Credit Corporation]]; the money financed the purchases of Iraq's military procurement network, including [[Matrix Churchill]] and the brass plant of [[SerVaas Inc.]][^1][^2] The [[Federal Bureau of Investigation|FBI]] raided the branch on August 4, 1989.[^3] The acting United States attorney in Atlanta, [[Joe D. Whitley]], recused himself on taking office on June 1, 1990, because his former law firm had represented Matrix Churchill.[^4] The indictment returned in Atlanta on February 28, 1991, named Drogoul and, among others, [[Safa al-Habobi]].[^5] Judge [[Marvin Shoob]] called Drogoul and his colleagues "pawns and bit-players in a far more wide-ranging conspiracy."[^6] The investigation became the [[BNL Scandal]].

### BCCI and the National Bank of Georgia

In January 1978 [[Ghaith R. Pharaon]] completed his purchase of the [[National Bank of Georgia]] from [[Bert Lance]], with the help of [[Agha Hasan Abedi]] of the [[Bank of Credit and Commerce International]], who paid off Lance's 3.5 million dollar bank loan the same day. [[First American Bank]], secretly owned by BCCI, bought the bank from Pharaon for 227 million dollars in 1987.[^7][^13]

### MKULTRA

After May 1943 [[George Hunter White]] of the [[Office of Strategic Services]] tested a truth drug on suspected Communist soldiers at camps near Atlanta, Memphis and [[New Orleans]].[^8] Under [[Project MKUltra|MKULTRA]] Subproject 47 the [[Central Intelligence Agency|CIA]] tested large doses of [[LSD]] on volunteers at the [[Atlanta Federal Penitentiary]] through [[Emory University]].[^9]

### Other Records

The [[Southern Christian Leadership Conference]], with [[Martin Luther King Jr.]] as president, was organized in Atlanta on January 10 and 11, 1957.[^10] [[George R. Wackenhut]] was an FBI special agent in Atlanta and Indianapolis from 1951 to 1954, before founding the [[Wackenhut Corporation]].[^11] The car [[James Earl Ray]] had driven from Memphis after the killing of King in April 1968 was found abandoned in Atlanta.[^12]
