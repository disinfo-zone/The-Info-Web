### Signals Intelligence

New Zealand's Government Communications Security Bureau is a member of the [[Five Eyes]] alliance under the [[UKUSA Agreement]] of 1946, with the [[National Security Agency]], [[GCHQ]], and the Australian and Canadian agencies.[^1] Its station at Waihopai was a collection site of [[ECHELON]], a network first described in detail in Nicky Hager's 1996 book *Secret Power: New Zealand's Role in the International Spy Network,* which drew on interviews with the bureau's employees.[^2]

### Operations and Attacks

In July 1985 agents of the French [[SDECE|DGSE]] sank the Greenpeace vessel *Rainbow Warrior* in Auckland harbor, killing a photographer; DGSE officers were convicted in New Zealand.[^3] On March 15, 2019, [[Brenton Tarrant]], an Australian who had lived about two years in Christchurch, attacked the Al Noor Mosque and the Linwood Islamic Centre there, killing 51 people; on March 26, 2020, he pleaded guilty to 51 murders, 40 attempted murders and a terrorist act, the first terrorism conviction in New Zealand, and he was sentenced to life without parole.[^4][^5] New Zealand designated [[The Base]] and the Proud Boys as terrorist organizations in June 2022, and the [[Order of Nine Angles]] in 2025.[^6][^7]

### Thiel's Citizenship

[[Peter Thiel]] was granted New Zealand citizenship on August 4, 2011, under an "exceptional circumstances" provision that waived the residency requirement, having visited the country four times and never lived there; the grant was disclosed in February 2017. He bought a 193-hectare estate near Lake Wanaka.[^8]

### Other Records

At the University of Otago in Dunedin, the psychologists [[David Marks]] and [[Richard Kammann]] began in 1975 an investigation of the remote-viewing experiments of [[Russell Targ]] and [[Hal Puthoff]] at [[Stanford Research Institute]], and reported in *Nature* in 1978 that cues in the transcripts let judges match them to the targets without visiting the sites.[^9] [[R.C. Horsch]] worked as a chemist in Timaru under a stolen identity until he was caught re-entering the country with five false passports in 1984.[^10]
