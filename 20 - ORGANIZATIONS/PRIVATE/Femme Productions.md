Femme Productions was an adult film company founded in 1984 by [[Candida Royalle]] to produce "adult films from a woman's perspective" and films that couples could watch together. It released 18 films between 1984 and 2007, 13 directed by Royalle, which she described as "sensuously explicit," with "high production values, non-formulaic eroticism."[^1][^2] Its titles included *Three Daughters* (1986), *Sensual Escape* (1991), *Revelations* (1992), *My Surrender* (1996) and *Stud Hunters* (2002).[^1] The Internet Adult Film Database credits "RC Hürsch," a name used by [[R.C. Horsch]], as director of *Lovers* (1993) and *Lovers 2* (1994) for the company.[^3] In the 1990s Femme's titles were distributed by [[Adam and Eve (company)|Adam and Eve]].[^1]
