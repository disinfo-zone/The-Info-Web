WiSC Enterprises, LLC is a company in [[Chantilly, Virginia]]. Its website states that it was "Founded in 2008 and veteran-owned" and lists program support in intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance (ISR), geospatial intelligence (GEOINT) standards, SI/SETA, advanced concept technology demonstrations and joint capability technology demonstrations, and NIST compliance. The site gives the unique entity identifier C1CDGX5PGKC1 and the CAGE code 571L0, and describes the firm as a mentor in the [[Department of Defense]] Mentor-Protege Program that "has successfully guided four protege companies."[^1]

### National Air and Space Intelligence Center posting

On June 8, 2026 WiSC listed a full-time "Program Management Analyst, Junior" position, valid through August 8, 2026, at [[Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]] near Dayton, Ohio. The posting, for work at the [[National Air and Space Intelligence Center]], states that "WiSC Enterprises is seeking a motivated and mission-focused Program Management Analyst, Junior to support intelligence and defense operations at the National Air and Space Intelligence Center (NASIC). This position supports high-priority national security initiatives involving Geospatial Intelligence (GEOINT), Measurement and Signature Intelligence (MASINT), Foreign Materiel Exploitation (FME), and Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance (ISR) mission areas." The position requires an "Active Top Secret (TS) clearance with SCI adjudication" and is marked "contingent upon contract award." Listed duties include supporting "technical exchanges" and "promoting awareness of intelligence research and development initiatives across the Air Force enterprise." The posting names no contract or program.[^2]

On May 6, 2026 the Department of Defense announced an $866,000,000 ceiling multiple-award contract for advisory and assistance services for the National Air and Space Intelligence Center (ASTRA), awarded to [[Apogee Engineering]], [[KBR Wyle Services]], [[Riverside Research Institute]] and [[Systems Planning and Analysis]]; WiSC is not among the named holders.[^4]

### Federal awards

USAspending.gov lists one prime record for WiSC, N6833517G0034 ("CYBER BOA"), a [[U.S. Navy]] basic ordering agreement dated March 30, 2017 with no obligated dollars. It lists one subaward, STM1241301-000, dated June 16, 2016, of $238,986.15 under [[Alion Science and Technology]] award 0085 for the [[Air Force Research Laboratory]] Information Directorate, whose description covers collection management and planning systems for sensors feeding the [[Defense Common Ground System]].[^3]
