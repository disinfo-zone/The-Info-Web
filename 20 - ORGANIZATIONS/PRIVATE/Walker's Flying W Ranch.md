Walker's Flying W Ranch, LLC is a Texas limited liability company with a taxpayer address in [[Sierra Blanca, Texas]] ([[Hudspeth County, Texas]]). The [[Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts]] franchise tax account status for taxpayer number 32033808372 gives its [[Texas Secretary of State]] file number as 0800874977, its effective registration date as September 24, 2007, and its right to transact business and its registration status as active. The Public Information Report for 2026 lists one manager, Dennis R. Walker, with the post office box that the Comptroller account gives as the company's mailing address.[^1]

### Radio licence in the Quitman Mountains

The [[Federal Communications Commission]] Universal Licensing System lists Walker's Flying W Ranch, LLC (licensee ID L02198220) as holder of call sign WRBZ759, an Industrial/Business Pool licence under rule part 90.35(a), granted August 3, 2018 and expiring August 3, 2028. The base station operates on 152.315 MHz, with 25 mobile units on 152.315 and 157.575 MHz. The listed location is a 4.6 meter pole in Hudspeth County at 31 10 29.5 N, 105 30 2.3 W, ground elevation 1,840 feet, in the [[Quitman Mountains]]. The FCC entity record gives a Sierra Blanca post office box and a contact e-mail address on the milehighresources.com domain; the box is the one that the Texas Comptroller records give for [[Mile High Resources]] and Mile High Ordnance, LLC, and differs from the one the Comptroller gives for Walker's Flying W Ranch, LLC itself (the Comptroller gives the latter's box for [[Charlie Helicopter Company]], LLC as well).[^2]

Computed from the licence coordinates and the centroid of the 838-acre parcel at 2000 Skunk Canyon (31.17794 N, 105.4835 W), the pole site lies 1.04 miles from the parcel whose address the Mile High Resources Range gives in Federal Communications Commission experimental filings. It lies about 8.5 miles from the coordinates (31 16 18 N, 105 24 46 W) that [[Raytheon]] gives for the range in its 2025 and 2026 telemetry filings.[^2][^3]

### International Boundary and Water Commission award

[[USAspending]] records award IBM14P0145 to Walker's Flying W Ranch, LLC (Sierra Blanca; flagged as a women-owned small business) from the [[International Boundary and Water Commission]], United States and Mexico, a component of the [[United States Department of State]]. It was signed September 10, 2014 and ran to August 31, 2019 (last modified August 29, 2018), for $13,301.40, with the description "AGREEMENT TO CONTINUE SERVICES TO ACCESS QUITMAN MTN SITE FOR MAINTENANCE OF RADIO TOWER AND EQUIPMENT FOR AMERICAN DAM FIELD OFFICE." The competition field reads "NOT COMPETED," with solicitation procedure "only one source" and one offer received; place of performance is coded El Paso. A parallel award, IBM14P0140 ($12,900.00, signed August 30, 2014, same description and competition fields), went to two individuals of El Paso.[^4]

The [[Drug Enforcement Administration]] [[El Paso Intelligence Center]] made two payments to the same two El Paso individuals, as recorded by USAspending for access to Quitman Mountain: DJD14NEP0008 ($2,582.88, signed January 7, 2014, running to November 30, 2014, "LICENSE RENEWAL OF ACCESS TO QUITMAN MOUNTAIN") and DJD15NEP0006 ($2,582.88, signed December 3, 2014, running to November 30, 2015, "RIGHT OF WAY LICENSE TO ACCESS REMOTE COLLECTION ON QUITMAN MOUNTAIN."). Both are recorded as "NOT COMPETED UNDER SAP," with place of performance coded Fort Bliss, El Paso County.[^5]

USAspending returns no other contract, grant or payment award to Walker's Flying W Ranch, LLC, and the [[System for Award Management]] public entity search returns no active registration for the company.[^4][^6]
