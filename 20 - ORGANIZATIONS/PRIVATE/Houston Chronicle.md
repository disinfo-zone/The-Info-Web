The Houston Chronicle is a daily newspaper in [[Houston]], [[Texas]]. On March 12, 1987, when it was the largest daily in Texas, with a paid circulation of 425,434 daily and 528,153 on Sunday, it was sold to the [[Hearst Corporation]] for 400 million dollars. Its owner, the nonprofit [[Houston Endowment]], had to give up its controlling interest in the paper by 1989 under federal law.[^1]

### BCCI and Bush reporting

On June 4, 1992 the Chronicle published Jerry Urban's "Bush Said Friend's Arbusto Investment Was His Own, Not Saudi Money," on the investment by [[James R. Bath]] in [[George W. Bush]]'s oil company [[Arbusto Energy]].[^2]
