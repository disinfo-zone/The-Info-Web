The Stanford Social Innovation Review (SSIR) is a quarterly on philanthropy, nonprofits and social enterprise at [[Stanford University]]. According to the [[Stanford Graduate School of Business]], its Center for Social Innovation, a grantee of the [[William and Flora Hewlett Foundation]], launched the review in the spring of 2003.[^1]

### Coverage of Open Philanthropy

The review's Summer 2018 issue carried "Giving in the Light of Reason" by [[Marc Gunther]], a profile of [[Cari Tuna]] and [[Open Philanthropy]] that counted seven entities in which Tuna served as a director or major donor, among them [[Good Ventures]], the [[Open Philanthropy Action Fund]] and [[GiveWell]], and quoted [[Alexander Berger]] that "tax considerations are key."[^2]
