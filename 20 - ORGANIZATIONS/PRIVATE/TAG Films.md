TAG Films was a video production company in Amsterdam. S. Dovey wrote that it was established by three Welsh pedophiles, [[Alan Williams]], [[John Gay]] and [[Lee Tucker]].[^1] [[Nick Davies]] wrote in 2000 that Williams, known as the Welsh Witch, arrived in Amsterdam in 1988 and managed [[Boys Club 21]] at 21 Spuistraat, and that across the road at number 44 [[Warwick Spinks]] ran a similar club, the [[Gay Palace]]; Davies wrote that both men "had much wider and crueller interests," and that Williams introduced Lee Tucker and John Gay, who "set up TAG Films."[^2] In a 1998 article Davies described Spinks as working at Boys For Men at 44b Spuistraat, and Williams as managing Boys Club 21 "across the road."[^3] The two Davies accounts name different clubs for Spinks; both are attributed. Dovey wrote that a Scotland Yard informant named "Frank" described how the group produced a snuff film inside a barn belonging to a German man who frequented the boy brothels managed by Williams and Spinks, and that the investigation into TAG Films was part of [[Operation Framework]], the joint Scotland Yard and Dutch authorities inquiry conducted between 1992 and 1993 targeting suspected snuff pornography produced by British nationals in Amsterdam.[^1]

Dovey wrote that TAG Films was linked to the Dusseldorf distributor [[Gero-Video]] and that a German raid on Gero-Video followed.[^4] Davies wrote that TAG Films sold its films "through distributors in the United States and Germany" and that German police raided "a video distributor in Dusseldorf" after Bristol detectives followed Tucker and Gay, without naming it.[^2] Der Spiegel reported in 1998 that investigators had found nothing punishable at Gero-Video and that proceedings had been discontinued.[^5]
