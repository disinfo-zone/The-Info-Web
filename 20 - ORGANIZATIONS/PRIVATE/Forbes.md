Forbes is an American business magazine and media company published by Forbes Media LLC. [[Steve Forbes]] is its chairman and editor-in-chief.[^1]

### Sale to Integrated Whale Media

On July 18, 2014 Forbes Media announced that a group of international investors would buy a majority stake, that the Forbes family would keep "a significant ownership stake," and that the private equity firm [[Elevation Partners]] would "fully exit its investment." Terms were not disclosed. The buyer, the newly formed [[Hong Kong]] group [[Integrated Whale Media Investments]] (IWM), was led by [[Integrated Asset Management]] (Asia) Limited, founded by [[Tak Cheung Yam]]; another significant investor was [[Wayne Hsieh]], co-founder of [[ASUSTeK Computer]], based in [[Singapore]]. Yam said: "As more market-based economies emerge globally, interest in the information that Forbes provides and the message it delivers resonates with a growing audience."[^1]

### Austin Russell bid

In May 2023 [[Austin Russell]], founder and chief executive of [[Luminar Technologies]], whom Forbes had named the "world's youngest self-made billionaire" in 2021, agreed to buy 82 percent of Forbes Global Media Holdings at a valuation of nearly 800 million dollars. He missed the November 1, 2023 closing deadline, and on November 21 IWM terminated the agreement. *[[TechCrunch]]* reported that investors including India's [[Sun Group]], whose vice chairman [[Shiv Khemka]] had "committed to invest $300 million," "didn't wire the money they were contractually obligated to."[^2]
