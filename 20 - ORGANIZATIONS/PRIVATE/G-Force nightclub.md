The G-Force was a nightclub in [[Amsterdam]] frequented by [[Marc Dutroux]] and [[Robbie Van Der Plancken]], according to a dossier compiled by the [[Morkhoven Workgroup]]. The Morkhoven Workgroup dossier, as reported by S. Dovey, identified the club's owner as an American named John Edward Mullaney and linked him through a company called [[Korper and Korver]] to [[John Stamford]]'s [[Spartacus Network]] and through a company called [[Adonis]] to [[Karel Maasdam]].[^1] The Dutch commercial register lists a J.E. Mullaney Holding B.V. (Chamber of Commerce number 33304101), registered in Amsterdam and now inactive; the initials match the name given in the dossier and the register entry does not state who held the company.[^2] The register also lists a Korper B.V. (65922646) and an Adonis B.V. (09208081), both active; no officer is shown for any of the three.[^2] The bar at the G-Force was operated by a bar manager.[^1]

According to witness Robert Jan Warmerdam, who frequented the small sadomasochist scene in Amsterdam for about 15 years, Dutroux had meetings at the G-Force on regular dates with an Amsterdam police official, as well as with a laboratory worker. Warmerdam claimed he had often been at table with them and that Dutroux had said he expected problems and was going to go to prison for a short time. Warmerdam also stated he met Robbie Van Der Plancken at the G-Force and later in [[Rotterdam|Rotterdam, Netherlands]]. The dossier linking Dutroux to the G-Force and Amsterdam child trafficking networks was compiled by [[Marcel Vervloesem]] for prosecutor [[Michel Bourlet]] and formed the basis for a series of 2004 media reports.[^1]
