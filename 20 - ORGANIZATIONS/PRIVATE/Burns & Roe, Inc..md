Burns & Roe, Inc. was an international engineering and construction corporation. It was a family corporation owned by [[Kenneth A. Roe]] and his family. According to his 1999 obituary, [[George K. Pender]] joined Burns & Roe, Inc. in [[Oradell, New Jersey|Oradell]], New Jersey, as a sales representative after working for [[Aerojet|General Aerojet Corporation]] in [[Los Angeles]], and was responsible for establishing a West Coast division for the firm.[^1] [[Cheri Seymour]] wrote that he was a former Director of the [[Pacific Ocean]] area for Burns & Roe, Inc., and a Senior Engineer Consultant to the company.[^2]

Under Kenneth A. Roe's leadership, Burns & Roe, Inc. was involved in major projects, including the engineering design and construction of the [[United States|U.S.A.]] Fast Breeder Nuclear Reactor Plant in conjunction with [[Westinghouse Electric Corporation]], which was responsible for the nuclear system supply of steam. At the time, the construction value of Burns & Roe, Inc.'s business backlog exceeded six billion U.S. dollars.[^2]
