CORU was "integrated by 5 anti-Castro groups which united in the [[Dominican Republic]] on June 11, 1976, under the leadership of Dr. [[Orlando Bosch]]": Bosch's Acción Cubana; the [[Cuban Nationalist Movement]], "a right wing organization which claimed credit for acts of violence in the United States and Canada during the mid-1960s"; the [[Frente de Liberación Nacional Cubano|Cuban National Liberation Front]], formed in October 1973 and credited with about 25 acts; the veterans' association of [[Brigade 2506]]; and the 17th of April Movement. Bosch told an interviewer from prison: "We were conspiring there. Planning bombings and killings. Everything was planned there."[^1][^2]

### 1976

In September Bosch reached [[Caracas]] under Venezuelan protection. On October 6 two employees of [[Luis Posada Carriles]] blew up a [[Cubana Flight 455 bombing|Cubana airliner]] off Barbados. [[Ricardo Morales Navarrete]] of [[DISIP]] named [[Frank Castro]], "a leader of CORU," among those who planned it at the Anauco Hilton, and said Castro had wanted his own front to claim it and then, after the deaths, did not. Members of the Cuban Nationalist Movement had meanwhile gone to Washington with [[Michael Townley]] of [[DINA]] and killed [[Orlando Letelier]] on September 21.[^3]

### After Bosch

With Bosch in a Caracas prison the leadership passed to Frank Castro, "CORU Chief from Miami." Informants reported him reorganizing cells, planning to sabotage a Mexican airliner on the Mérida-Havana route while insisting "that all operations must be outside of the boundaries of the United States," raising money among exile doctors for a jailbreak in the Dominican Republic, and running a "young action oriented cell" of [[Alvin Ross]] and Raciel Rodríguez that bombed the Viasa Airlines office in Miami Beach on December 23, 1977. In January 1977 a CORU member came to Miami on the instructions of Venezuela's [[Orlando García Vásquez]] to arrange "a dramatic mission that would cause maximum publicity," a naval attack on Cuban shipping, and a lesser bombing, to "take away the 'pressure' from Orlando Bosch." In January 1978 [[Guillermo Novo]] was organizing cells in [[Union City, New Jersey]] "for military action in the U.S. and against the country of Venezuela." Bosch wanted "war declared against Venezuela." In July 1978 Bosch, from prison, removed Castro and named Dr. Carlos Dominicis interim head. An informant reported an offer of money from the [[Unification Church]] to a Chicago federation of which CORU was proposed as the military arm.[^1][^4]

The Justice Department wrote in 1989 that CORU "has claimed responsibility for numerous bombings in Miami, New York, Venezuela, Panama, Mexico, Argentina, and elsewhere."[^5]
